Daily Targets Aging Athlete
Protein (1.8g/kg × ~75kg)~135g/day
Vitamin D (supplement)2,000–4,000 IU
Creatine monohydrate3–5g/day
Water (hard training day)3–4 L
Protein is key for power in aging athletes — Roche calls this out specifically. Spread across 4–5 meals (max ~40g absorbed per sitting). Creatine has strong evidence for muscle mass and cognitive function in 40+ athletes.
Workout Day Tue & Sat
1–2 hrs before
Oatmeal (½ cup dry) + banana + drizzle of honey
Or: toast + jam + small nut butter (light fat)
Or: rice + banana (GI-friendly backup)
Keep fat and fiber low close to the workout
During (hills > 45 min)
30–60g carbs/hr: dates, banana, gels
2–3 medjool dates = ~45g carbs — real food works great
Electrolytes every 30–45 min, especially sodium
Within 30–45 min after
20–40g protein + 40–60g carbs
Greek yogurt (1 cup) + fruit + granola
2–3 eggs + toast + fruit
Chocolate milk + banana (simple, effective)
Long Run Day Saturday
2 hrs before
Oatmeal + banana + 2 eggs
Or: rice bowl + egg + light protein
Practice race-day nutrition habits on long runs
During (from ~30 min in)
60g carbs/hr: dates, banana, boiled potato + salt

Recovery meal (1–2 hrs after)
Salmon or chicken + rice + cooked vegetables
Sweet potato is excellent — easy to digest, anti-inflammatory
Tart cherry juice reduces muscle damage and soreness
Easy & Rest Days Mon / Wed / Fri
Approach
Don't under-eat on rest days — protein target stays the same
Slightly fewer carbs, more healthy fats and vegetables
Focus on anti-inflammatory whole foods
Best choices
Salmon or sardines (omega-3s for inflammation + recovery)
Leafy greens: spinach, kale, arugula
Berries: blueberries, tart cherries (antioxidants)
Avocado, olive oil, walnuts
Eggs: 3 at breakfast + Greek yogurt or cottage cheese

GI-friendly rules (your history)
Rice over bread/pasta during training weeks

Monday Heat Protocol Every Monday
Why it matters
Sauna or hot tub improves blood volume — real aerobic adaptation
15–20 min at ~170°F sauna or hot tub is sufficient
2–3 sessions per week provides meaningful adaptation
Hydration around heat
Drink 16–24 oz water or electrolyte drink before
Replace at least equal volume after
Pinch of salt in water if sweating heavily
